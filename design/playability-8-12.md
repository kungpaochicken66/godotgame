# Play ideas for ages 8-12: implementation and decisions

Source: the reference package `Lantern_Lane_Playability_8-12_EN.zip` (SHA-256 `4cc62502…e225cf`; extracted for reading into the git-ignored `tools/reference/`). It is treated as reference data: where it differs from the real game, the game wins. Status: 2026-10-07. Batch 1 (r9, frozen as `ipad-r9`), batch 2 (r10, `ipad-r10`) and the r11 extension (the earlier deferred parts and batch-2 follow-ups; frozen as `ipad-r11`, manifest `f22891d4…e19b1`, after the final EC2 regression) implemented and checked headless on EC2 only, uncommitted, on top of r8c (pets, themed rooms, cooking, Photos, invites). All ten ideas have a playable slice; the package is not fully complete (see "Not done and why"). Rendered, touch and device acceptance of r9, r10 and r11 are pending (Mac); none of them is installed or deployed.

Principles kept from both the package and the game: everything free and available from the start; no money, scores, timers, hunger or neglect penalties; joining is always a choice; creations stay; six languages; iPad and iPhone touch; solo and 2-4 players with the authority deciding; saves stay compatible.

## How batch 1 was built (actual project first)

## r11: the deferred parts and batch-2 follow-ups (actual project first)

Order: invitations first (other slices offer them), then animal games, weather toys, blueprints, then the small follow-ups. Protocol 7 → 8.

Closeout fix (found in `net_test.py --r11`, reproduced deterministically): a **different** invitation sent within 3 s of another was refused ("Your friends have your invitation.") because the cooldown was per inviter. It now only stops repeating the same invitation (same kind and target); a different one goes out and, one card per inviter, replaces the earlier card. Three kinds no button ever sent (track, boat, leaves) were removed from the list.

r11b (after the r11 Mac image review): the paper boat's lap follows the pond's water ellipse and hops over the rim; Tumble's fountain faces the viewer with larger droplets; handheld props stay upright through emotes and poses; the music box "Bell" row is translated in all six languages; the Play button shows a stop mark while playing.

## Not done and why

The reference package is **not** fully complete. Still deferred after r11, with reasons:

- F02: per-friend switch control and "deliver to a friend's door" routes (the ball follows pieces only); a toy face that opens its mouth is drawn as a catching cup.
- F03: Biscuit's tail-first return, Pip lifting a foot first, and passing the ball between children (animation detail; fetch takes turns instead); Wooly's rhythm is emote-based, not musical call and response.
- F04: snowball sizes, star-shaped splashes, a "quieter scene" toggle for effects (the existing Sounds switch and music box mute cover sound only).
- F05: cardboard pieces are free-standing props (no "stage mode"); captions are untested without a renderer.
- F06: icon clues are the four emotes only (no "pass a bridge" or "sit" clues).
- F07: Modeling lane wearables (full equip integration is a separate, evidenced handoff; rig contract in docs/kid-rig.md); magnifier bubble and baton effects.
- F08: the 4 x 8 grid is one shared loop; no "join at a natural musical moment" timing aid.
- F09: blueprints stay on one device (no sharing between friends) and leave out wall and ceiling things.
- F10: a separate "Playing together" scene icon; selecting specific friends to invite (invitations go to everyone in town).
- New modeled assets: none requested from the Modeling lane yet; the new props are code-built in the existing procedural style and can be replaced through the catalog `model` path.
