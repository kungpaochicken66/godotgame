# Lantern Lane: experience plan (owner's working record)

Started 2026-11-13 by the EC2 source owner at the user's direction. This is the plan and the ranked issue list for bringing Lantern Lane to a polished, coherent candidate within its existing creative-town scope. It is updated as work proceeds; statuses say what is verified and what is only believed.

## Who it is for

An eight-year-old beginner with no building experience, playing by touch on an iPad or iPhone or with one controller on an Apple TV, alone or with up to three friends. They should understand the game without help, start playing, and want to keep creating.

## Targets (design and acceptance targets, not claims)

| # | Target | Verified how |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | **First minute:** discover what to do and finish a first build easily | A scripted first-minute tour (headless for logic; the Mac for pictures); in the end, a child |
| T2 | **First five minutes:** build or decorate a small home or place, meet and interact with the animals, see clear feedback | The same, plus the existing animal tours |
| T3 | **Next half hour:** understandable optional goals, surprises and creative combinations, with a discoverable way into the eight slices | Guide and toy box checks; cross-feature tests; a person |
| T4 | **Recovery:** undo or safely correct anything; no stuck menu, trapped child or stranded rider; leaving, rejoining and reloading keep what was meant to be kept | Authority and save tests, real clients, the tours' B and Cancel checks |
| T5 | **Presentation:** scene, things, UI, sound and feedback are coherent; exactly en, zh-CN, ja, es, fr, de; touch and controller, with the Apple TV's Home and input safety kept | Repository checks, six-language tours, the Mac's rendered originals, devices |

Passing automation shows function. It does not show that a child can use or enjoy something. Three judgments are kept apart everywhere: **functional correctness** (EC2 can test), **visual quality** (needs the Mac's rendered originals) and **interaction quality** (needs a device and a person).

**Echo defaults (chosen by the coordinator within its delegated routine decisions and relayed on 2026-11-13; not reviewed or accepted by the user personally):** one echo per player per town; a new one replaces the old; three emotes from the fixed list; one answer per friend, bounded by the town's capacity; the creator can delete it; it expires after seven days; fixed-list names and emotes only; no recorded audio, no free text, no real-world tracking; no accounts or outside services. Expiry, deletion and save and load behavior are documented and tested.

## Phases

1. **Freeze the current slice.** Finish the producer checks, seal `puzzle-rides` r1 with its request for the Mac, and never change it again.
2. **Complete the connected play loop.** Slices 3 to 8 in this order: lamplighter walk, signal rails, weather toys, picture board, friend echoes, animal passengers. One follow-on candidate based on r1.
3. **Polish.** The loop below, on controls, discovery, feedback and presentation.
4. **Stabilize and seal.** Final candidates with docs, manifest hashes and an exact request for the Mac. Stop at READY_FOR_LOCAL_VALIDATION.

Working loop: look at the experience as it is → name the highest-impact playability problem → fix it → test and observe → update the list below → again. Every extra change says which player problem it solves.

Out of scope: an open world, an economy, a large quest system, accounts, anything in another project. Not authorized: commits, pushes, spending, release, signing, installation, HK changes, credential or provider changes.

## Candidate `first-steps`: design as first written (implemented; the design of record is [first-steps.md](first-steps.md))

Two changes, both on the device only (no protocol or save change):

1. **First steps.** For a child who has not done them on this device, one short line at a time near the top of the screen, with a small picture and a way to hide it: (a) "Tap the ground to walk there." (with a controller: "Move with the left stick."), done after walking a few steps; (b) "Tap Decorate to build something.", then, with the toy box open, "Pick a thing, then tap Place here.", done when the first thing is placed; (c) "Tap Activities for ideas to play.", done when the guide is opened. Then one sentence of praise and never again. Kept in the device's settings, like the other preferences. Hidden while a dialog is open. Player problem solved: issue 1.
2. **Guide entries** for the new play: contraptions, puzzle rooms, cart rides, and a hello for friends, each with a picture drawn in code like the eight existing ones, the steps in the buttons' own words, and a "Go there" that opens Decorate on the right toy box tab. Player problem solved: issue 10.

Both need: a pure, unit-tested state for the first steps; a tour by touch and controller in six languages with a fresh user-data folder (and a second start that shows nothing); the Mac's pictures for how the line and the new guide pictures look; and, in the end, a child.
