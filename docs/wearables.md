# Wearables in the game (r12, r12b, r14)

Integration report for the Modeling lane's wearable clothing in the actual game. Model acceptance (the lab's frozen batches and `evidence/wear-r2/WEAR_R2_ACCEPTANCE.md`), in-game acceptance (this page, EC2 tests and the Mac run of candidate `ipad-r12`) and device acceptance (real touch on iPhone/iPad, not done) are kept separate.

## What is in the game

- **434 accepted wearables, 0 gated.** Every id comes from a FROZEN batch whose `BATCH_MANIFEST.json` SHA-256 matches its `FROZEN` record and the expected digest, and every GLB matches its manifest hash (`scripts/build_wear_catalog.py`, `--check` verifies only). The GLBs are copied byte-for-byte to `game/assets/wearables/<id>.glb`; the catalog is `game/data/wearables.json` (name, category, slot, band, held, hold record, seated variant, part names, source batch, SHA-256, triangles).
- **Seated variants:** the 61 skirted garments use their wear-r2 rev4 versions (c016 43, c017 18), which replace the same ids in c003/c011; the superseded baseline GLBs are not used.
- **Held items (60):** usable only with the W9 hold contract: 56 through the frozen wear-r2 rev5 hold records c018/c019 (final acceptance 2026-10-08; their GLBs are checked to be the same bytes as c014/c013) and the 4 accepted pilot items. A held item without a record would be listed as gated, shown as "still being checked" in its tab and refused by the authority (the mechanism is unit-tested with an injected entry; none is gated now).
- **Not used (excluded):** the retired v2-only batches c001/c002/c005/c008/c009, the pilot batch c015, the 43 c003 and 18 c011 standing-only versions that c016/c017 replace, and the 16 clothing references the lab blocked for third-party IP (Champion outfit, Champion wig, Cinnamoroll hat, Cinnamoroll sneakers, Force ReBoots, Hello Kitty shoes, Hero Boot Replicas, Hylian boots, Kerokerokeroppi boots, Kiki & Lala shoes, Kerokerokeroppi socks, Kiki & Lala socks, Luigi 'stache, Mario 'stache, Hero's sword and shield, Ink tank). Blocked references are not unlocked or substituted.

Source batches (BATCH_MANIFEST SHA-256 prefix): c003-dressup `aa7993b4`, c004-headwear `bc169605`, c006-socks `0174d53a`, c007-accessories `d83eaed1`, c010-tops-v3 `60fe011c`, c011-bottoms-v3 `9a3f42bc`, c012-shoes-v3 `09a6c538`, c013-bags-v3 `7eefb238`, c014-umbrellas-r2 `eeb283a7`, c016-dressup-sit `0629dab9`, c017-skirts-sit `46b00a63`, c018-umbrellas-hold `9beac0ca`, c019-bags-hold `8eaa1fa3`.

## Player experience

- **Dress up -> Wardrobe** (free, no unlocks): nine category tabs (Tops, Bottoms, Dress-up, Headwear, Accessories, Socks, Shoes, Bags, Umbrellas), a scrolling two-column list sorted by name in the player's language, each tile showing the whole name (wrapped, never clipped; full names are unique per tab in every language), a check mark on what is worn, **Take it off** per tab, Back to Dress up. Names and tabs are translated into the six languages. The list takes only the height the safe area leaves and refits whenever the card's layout changes (fonts settling, wrapped tabs and names, a language change, the safe area), so the card stays on screen (r12b: the r12 Mac run showed a German card measured once, before its tabs wrapped, at 985 px on a 969 px screen).
- **Pictures (r14):** each tile shows the item's own picture above its whole name. The picture is rendered on demand from the item's frozen GLB, as worn by a standing child (seated variants hidden), framed to its own bounds (`game/scripts/ui/wear_thumbs.gd`).
  - **Cost:** nothing at app startup. The picture maker is created the first time the Wardrobe opens; only the rows on screen plus one row ahead are rendered.
  - **Changes:** scrolling, resizing and tab changes replace the wanted list, so passed-over tabs and rows are not rendered (at most the one picture already under way finishes into the cache).
  - **Limits:** at most 120 pictures stay in memory (least recently used dropped first; rows on screen are kept), and one 128 px render viewport exists only while pictures are being made; it is freed after 2 s idle.
  - **Failures:** a model that cannot be pictured is skipped, not retried.
  - **Scroll position (r14b):** a reopened tab returns to the remembered position once the list is laid out (re-applied each frame until stable, bounded); only then are pictures asked for and is the card marked settled.
  - **Headless:** without a renderer, a flat per-item colour stands in, so tests exercise the same rules.
- **Kept per device:** the look (including `wear`) is saved in the device settings like the hat and colors, comes back on the next join and on Reconnect (Reconnect now rejoins with the current look, not the look from the first join; this also fixes hats changed in town).
- **Shared:** friends see the outfit; it travels in the avatar (`ev_avatar`) and in every join snapshot.

## Rules (EQUIP_HANDOFF CURRENT STATUS and section 9)

- **Slots:** top, bottom, dress (occupies top and bottom: a dress-up clears both; a top or bottom clears the dress-up), head, face, socks, shoes, bag (worn bags), hand (umbrellas and hand-held bags). Headwear and the procedural hat exclude each other; a held item and the procedural handheld prop exclude each other (`Avatar.resolve`, the authority's `dress` and `wear`).
- **Hides:** top hides the baked torso outfit and sleeves; dress hides torso, sleeves and trouser cuffs; bottom hides the lower outfit (below y 0.5) and cuffs; shoes hide the baked shoes; non-band headwear hides all hair. The 15 band-style headwear (crowns, flower bands, clips, ears, boppers) keep the hair. Hidden parts are not built at all (no merged-mesh surgery).
- **Mount:** each `<id>_<Pivot>[_sit]` mesh is re-parented under the child's pivot (Body, Head, ArmL, ArmR, LegL, LegR) with an identity transform after every `kid.setup()`; the old meshes go with the old rig, so there is never a duplicate. GLB scenes are cached (at most 40, least recently used first).
- **Per pose:** in sit, rest, ride and swing a garment with a seated variant shows `Body_sit` + `LegL_sit` + `LegR_sit` and hides `Body` (never both). In sit/rest with a seated variant both arms go to x -1.1 (over the knees) and a held item is hidden. Held items hide in ride and swing; bags hide while seated; an umbrella may show in sit/rest only without a seated variant. Otherwise the right arm stays in the hold envelope (x -0.6..0.6, z 0..0.05) and emotes play on the left arm (mirrored) instead; the held item is restored when the state ends.
- **Authority:** `rq_action("wear", [slot, id])` (protocol 9). The server refuses unknown slots ("That is not a place to wear things."), gated items ("That one is not ready to wear yet.") and ids that are not accepted or not for that slot ("That is not in the wardrobe."); joins and saved looks are sanitized the same way (older saves without `wear` load unchanged).

## Remaining limits

- From the model acceptance: rigid per-pivot parts (no skinning), raised-arm sleeve shoulder step and sleeves near the face in swing, simplified seated fronts, hairstyle clearance of held items checked analytically. No device, performance or art acceptance.
- Protocol 9: the deployed HK server and the installed r11d apps are protocol 8; r12 apps need one synchronized, authorized server update (a protocol 8 peer is refused kindly).
- Pictures are made on the device the first time a row is shown. The cold budget is 3 s for the rows on screen; reopening a tab is served from the cache (budget 0.25 s, no new renders). The pictures are plain renders of the frozen models, not hand-made icons.
